🅿️ Kerb

Free, instant London parking answers from open data — the demo front-end for the Kerb MCP server.

Type a London place to search — answers come from a local snapshot of OpenStreetMap, so they're instant. Yellow lines: tick the overlay under the map (City of London lines, Barnet streets), or search a Barnet street name (e.g. Squires Lane) to see it highlighted on the map with its yellow-line data.
━━ double yellow — no waiting at any time · ━━ single yellow — no waiting at the hours in its tooltip · ┅┅ restriction published without hours — check the sign. Tap a line for details.
━━━ thick translucent line = a Barnet street with waiting restrictions: the whole street is highlighted, because Barnet publishes lengths per street and side, not the exact position of each painted line. Tap the street for its totals.
Open data is incomplete — coverage varies street by street and tariffs often aren't published openly. Always check the sign on the street; it is the legal truth. Data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via the Geofabrik Greater London extract. Bay and car park data with tariffs, times and how to pay: © Camden, Westminster, Brent and Ealing councils. Yellow-line (waiting restriction) data: © City of London Corporation, published as INSPIRE open data; street-level waiting data © London Borough of Barnet (open data portal). D-TRO (DfT) data joins the same engine once our data-consumer account is approved. Demo build v0.40.2.
