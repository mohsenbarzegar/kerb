🅿️ Kerb

Free, instant London parking answers from open data — the demo front-end for the Kerb MCP server.

Type a London place to search — answers come from a local snapshot of OpenStreetMap, so they're instant.
Open data is incomplete — coverage varies street by street and tariffs often aren't published openly. Always check the sign on the street; it is the legal truth. Data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via the Geofabrik Greater London extract. Bay and car park data with tariffs, times and how to pay: © Camden, Westminster, Brent and Ealing councils. D-TRO (DfT) data joins the same engine once our data-consumer account is approved. Demo build v0.19.0.
